##### &#x20;                                     **Sales Data Analysis and Dashboard**





###### &#x20;1. Project Overview



This project analyzes sales transaction data using Microsoft Excel. It explores overall sales performance, profitability, monthly sales trends, country-wise sales, salesperson performance, and product category contributions through an interactive dashboard.



###### &#x20;2. Project Objectives



\- Analyze total sales, average sales, profit, and order count.

\- Compare sales performance across countries.

\- Identify monthly sales trends.

\- Evaluate salesperson performance.

\- Understand sales contributions by product category.

\- Present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.



###### &#x20;3. Dataset Description



The dataset contains sales transaction information, including order dates, customers, countries, product categories, salespeople, sales amounts, and other available order details.



###### &#x20;4. Tools Used



\- Microsoft Excel

\- Excel Tables and formulas

\- Power Query

\- Pivot Tables

\- Pivot Charts

\- Slicers



###### &#x20;5. Steps Performed



1\. Reviewed the sales dataset.

2\. Checked data types and handled data-quality issues where appropriate.

3\. Prepared the data for analysis using Excel and Power Query.

4\. Created Pivot Tables to summarize sales performance.

5\. Analyzed country-wise sales, monthly trends, salesperson performance, and category-wise sales.

6\. Created KPI cards, charts, and interactive slicers.

7\. Documented business insights and recommendations.



###### &#x20;6. Dashboard Features



\- Total Sales KPI

\- Total Profit KPI

\- Total Orders KPI

\- Average Sales KPI

\- Country-wise Sales Chart

\- Monthly Sales Trend Chart

\- Sales by Salesperson Chart

\- Sales by Category Chart

\- Interactive filters for country, order date, salesperson, and category

###### &#x20;8. Key Business Insights



\- Total sales were ₹20,50,000.

\- Total profit was ₹4,10,000.

\- India recorded the highest sales among the countries displayed.

\- Electronics represented approximately 56% of category sales.

\- Australia recorded the lowest sales among the countries displayed.

\- Office Supplies represented approximately 17% of category sales.

\- Monthly sales fluctuated across the months displayed.

\- Alekhya had the highest sales among the salespeople displayed.

\- Priya had the lowest sales among the salespeople displayed.



###### &#x20;9. Business Recommendations



\- Investigate the factors contributing to India's strong sales performance.

\- Study the Electronics category's contribution and maintain suitable product availability.

\- Investigate lower sales in Australia and Office Supplies.

\- Review monthly sales patterns to support inventory and promotion planning.

\- Explore opportunities to improve salesperson performance through training and sharing effective practices.

###### &#x20;11. Project Outcomes



Developed an Excel-based sales analysis report that summarizes business performance, highlights sales patterns, and identifies opportunities for further investigation and improvement.
